GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Have another question? Our team is happy to help.

Contact us ↗
How does rent to buy work?

You choose a vehicle and agree a payment arrangement with the provider. The deposit, weekly amount, duration and conditions for ownership should all be set out in your agreement.

Can I view a vehicle before deciding?

Contact our team to discuss the vehicle and arrange a viewing where available.

Are servicing, road tax and MOT included?

What's included depends on the specific agreement. Ask our team for the full written breakdown before you commit.

Do you carry out credit checks?

Eligibility and any checks depend on the provider's terms. Contact us to discuss your circumstances and receive accurate information.

What happens at the end of the agreement?

Your agreement will explain the conditions for ownership, final payments and any other options available to you.
